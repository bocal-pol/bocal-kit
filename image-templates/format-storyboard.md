# Storyboard

## Effect
Sequence of film-storyboard-style narrative panels, with shot/camera annotations.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Grid of 4 to 6 numbered panels
- Simple sketches per panel, black-and-white pencil style
- Camera movement arrows + short notes under each panel

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`film storyboard of {{subject}}, grid of 4-6 numbered panels, simple sketch illustrations,
camera movement arrows and short direction notes below each panel, black and white pencil
sketch style`

## Negative Prompt
`single finished illustration, color photo, missing panel numbers, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Sequence/scene to break down?
2. Number of shots/panels?

## Combinable with
- `comic`, `handwritten`

## Example
Subject "15s product ad" → 5 panels: wide shot, product close-up, usage, logo, CTA.
