# Handwritten notes

## Effect
Notebook-style annotations, freehand arrows and circles, note-taking style.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Lined/graph paper background
- Simulated handwriting, freehand arrows/circles/underlines
- A few small doodles, black ink on white/cream paper

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`handwritten notebook page explaining {{subject}}, notebook lined paper background, hand-drawn
arrows circles and underlines, casual handwriting style annotations, small doodles, black ink
on white/cream paper`

## Negative Prompt
`typed text, digital font, printed layout, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Subject/concept to annotate?
2. Desired number of annotations/arrows?

## Combinable with
- `stickynotes`, `infographic`, `mindmap`

## Example
Subject "explaining an AI agent" → handwritten diagram with "input → LLM → tool call → output" arrows.
