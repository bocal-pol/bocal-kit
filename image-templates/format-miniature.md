# Miniature diorama — Tilt-shift

## Effect
Miniaturized diorama-style scene, tilt-shift effect, extreme depth of field.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Strong tilt-shift blur at top/bottom of frame, narrow focus band
- Saturated colors, slightly elevated angle
- Everything looks like a small-scale model

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`tilt-shift miniature diorama photo of {{subject}}, strong selective blur top and bottom of
frame, narrow focus band, saturated colors, slightly elevated angle, everything looks like a
tiny scale model`

## Negative Prompt
`full depth of field in focus, no tilt-shift blur, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Scene/place to miniaturize?
2. Shot angle (top-down / slightly angled)?

## Combinable with
- `tinyworkers`, `knolling`

## Example
Subject "city at sunset" → tilt-shift top-down view, miniature cars/buildings.
