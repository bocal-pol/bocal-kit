# Bricks — Lego

## Effect
Rebuilds the subject out of colorful modular building bricks.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Subject entirely made of visible rectangular bricks with studs
- Primary color palette
- Flat baseplate, toy playset photography style

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`{{subject}} rebuilt entirely out of colorful modular building bricks, visible stud connectors,
primary color palette, flat baseplate, toy playset photography style, studio lighting`

## Negative Prompt
`smooth realistic surfaces, no visible bricks, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Subject to rebuild in bricks?
2. Detail level (simple silhouette / highly detailed)?

## Combinable with
- `figurine`, `isometric`

## Example
Subject "Eiffel Tower" → brick reconstruction on a green baseplate.
