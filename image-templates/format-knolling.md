# Knolling — Grid flat lay

## Effect
Aligns all of the subject's elements/accessories at right angles in a grid, strict top-down view.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Strict top-down view
- Perfect grid alignment, even spacing
- Even flat lighting, neutral surface

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`knolling flat lay of {{subject}} and its accessories, perfectly arranged at right angles in a
grid pattern, shot directly from above, neutral flat surface, even studio lighting, minimalist
organized composition, high detail product photography`

## Negative Prompt
`messy arrangement, perspective angle, uneven lighting, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Subject and its accessories to include?
2. Desired surface/background (light wood, white, gray)?

## Combinable with
- `specsheet`

## Example
Subject "travel kit" → passport, earbuds, charger, notebook aligned in a grid on white.
