# Isometric view

## Effect
3D view at an isometric angle (30°), no perspective distortion, video-game/technical look.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- 30°/30°/90° axes, no vanishing point
- Consistent soft shadows
- Clean geometric shapes, often a limited palette

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`isometric view of {{subject}}, 30 degree axonometric projection, no perspective distortion,
clean geometric shapes, soft consistent shadows, video game asset / technical illustration
style, limited harmonious color palette`

## Negative Prompt
`perspective distortion, vanishing point, photographic depth of field, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Exact subject?
2. Style (stylized video game / clean technical)?

## Combinable with
- `wireframe`, `blueprint`, `knolling`

## Example
Subject "small country house" → management-game-style isometric view.
