# Tiny workers

## Effect
Hyper-realistic miniature workers building/repairing/shaping a giant-scale subject, macro photo.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Subject at a giant scale relative to the workers
- Realistic miniature tools/scaffolding/cranes
- Macro lighting, shallow depth of field, 8K detail

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`hyper-realistic miniature construction workers building/repairing giant {{subject}}, tiny
people using realistic tools scaffolding ladders and cranes, macro photography style, shallow
depth of field, 8K detail, dramatic lighting`

## Negative Prompt
`normal scale workers, cartoon style, watermark, blurry, low detail`

## Variables to collect
1. Giant subject (object, food, device)?
2. Workers' action (building / repairing / decorating)?

## Combinable with
- `miniature`, `macro` modifier

## Example
Subject "chocolate bar" → workers pouring molten chocolate with a miniature crane.
