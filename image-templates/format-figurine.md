# Figurine collector

## Effect
Turns the subject into a collector figurine with visible packaging, collectible product style.

Also covers the popular **actionfigure** (articulated figure with accessories), **funko**
(stylized big-head chibi vinyl style), and **toybox** (open toy box) variants — specify the
variant in Variable 2.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Stylized figurine of the subject on a display stand
- Blister/box packaging with clear window and a generic placeholder logo
- Studio product lighting, detailed plastic/vinyl texture

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`collectible figurine of {{subject}}, stylized toy sculpt on display stand, retail blister
packaging box with clear window and generic logo placeholder beside it, studio product
photography lighting, high detail plastic/vinyl texture`

## Negative Prompt
`unpackaged only, realistic human proportions, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Subject to turn into a figurine?
2. Variant (big-head chibi vinyl / articulated action figure / static vinyl)?

## Combinable with
- `knolling`, `lego`

## Example
Subject "house cat" → chibi vinyl style figurine with "Collector Series" box.
