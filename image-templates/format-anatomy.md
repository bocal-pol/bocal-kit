# Anatomy diagram

## Effect
Deconstructs the subject into stacked/floating sections with labels, editorial magazine style.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Floating horizontal sections, spaced vertically
- Callout labels on each section
- Clean background, editorial illustration style

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`anatomy diagram of {{subject}}, deconstructed into floating horizontal layers with labeled
callouts, editorial magazine illustration style, clean background, high detail`

## Negative Prompt
`assembled whole object only, no labels, cluttered background, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Composite subject (dish, garment, layered object)?
2. Number of layers to distinguish?

## Combinable with
- `crosssection`, `infographic`

## Example
Subject "gourmet burger" → stacked bun, sauce, patty, cheese, lettuce with labels.
