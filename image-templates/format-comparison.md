# Comparison

## Effect
Side-by-side presentation contrasting two elements/options.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Image cleanly split into two halves
- Each half labeled with a title
- Consistent lighting between both halves, symmetrical layout

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`side-by-side comparison of two {{subject}} options, image split into two clearly labeled
halves, symmetrical layout, consistent lighting on both sides, clean background`

## Negative Prompt
`asymmetric unbalanced layout, missing labels, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. The two elements being compared?
2. Comparison criteria to show as labels?

## Combinable with
- `beforeafter`, `infographic`

## Example
Subject "old vs new logo" → left half old logo, right half new one.
