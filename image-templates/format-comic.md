# Comic book page

## Effect
Inked comic book page, multiple panels, speech bubbles, halftone shading.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Grid of irregular panels
- Bold ink outlines, flat colors or halftone dots
- Speech bubbles with short text

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`comic book page about {{subject}}, multiple irregular panels, bold ink outlines, flat
cel-shaded colors with halftone dots, speech bubbles with short dialogue, dynamic comic
composition`

## Negative Prompt
`single panel, photorealistic rendering, no ink outlines, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Scene/short story to depict?
2. Number of panels and dialogue to include?

## Combinable with
- `storyboard`, `handwritten`

## Example
Subject "a developer's day" → 4 panels: coffee, bug, fix, successful deployment.
